The opportunity

The existing site already offers a foundation: room-based services, a substantial gallery, direct contact details, a consultation form, and 3D rendering services. The opportunity is to make that information feel more cohesive, easier to browse, and more useful to a homeowner deciding whether to call.

What I observed

  • The homepage repeats phone calls to action across several similar sections.
  • Social links on the homepage point to the general Instagram, Facebook, and Google sites rather than verified business profiles.
  • The testimonial is attributed to “Satisfied Homeowner,” and the homepage rating lacks a visible source link.
  • Many kitchen gallery items are presented as “Video,” without a descriptive project story.
  • The site has compelling 3D services, but project photos and concepts could be distinguished more clearly.

What this concept changes

  • A cinematic black-and-gold identity, the existing logo, bold typography, and a clearer page sequence.
  • Room filters and expandable portfolio highlights with descriptive image labels.
  • One consistent project-starting action, plus direct phone and email access.
  • A simple explanation of the proposed customer journey and practical FAQs.
  • A guided inquiry draft that collects project type, town, timing, and contact and language preferences.

These are observations from the public website, not a technical audit of the existing business systems. Conversion improvements are a design hypothesis to measure after launch; no traffic, leads, or revenue uplift is claimed.

Updated after a second review

Bilingual access

The homepage, service details, portfolio, FAQs, navigation, inquiry fields, validation, and draft messages are available in English and Spanish. The language choice is remembered, and an open inquiry is preserved when switching.

A more complete portfolio

The original gallery contains 27 videos and 10 still images. All 37 are included with category filters and a larger viewer. Videos load on demand. Original video audio and embedded text remain unchanged; the surrounding interface is translated.

Easier mobile contact, lighter images

A visible phone link, text-message links, a mobile contact bar, and expanded service descriptions reduce unnecessary steps. Images use lightweight WebP files, and videos load only when opened. The full-screen photo header keeps the page focused on the company’s work.

Next improvements require real business input

Connect and test real inquiry delivery; confirm who can handle Spanish inquiries; obtain attributable reviews, approved project stories, and photo permissions; then prepare the official domain, search indexing, and agreed measurement. These are the next launch priorities, rather than claims already made by this preview.

What is ready—and what needs owner input

Working in this concept

  • Responsive homepage and mobile navigation.
  • Six portfolio highlights plus the complete 37-item photo and video gallery, with room filters.
  • Service disclosures, FAQs, and clear contact links.
  • Three-step inquiry preparation, editable draft, copy action, and email-app handoff.
  • Complete English and Spanish pages, a visible language selector, and this bilingual proposal.

Before a client launch

  • Owner approval of brand, copy, process, service area, and photo rights.
  • Verified credentials, review links, project details, and any warranty claims.
  • Real team photos and authentic before-and-after pairs.
  • Connected form delivery, spam controls, and a suitable privacy notice.
  • Domain access, migration plan, analytics choices, and final acceptance.

This preview is a design proposal for owner review, not the contractor’s official site. It deliberately does not submit leads, track visitors, invent reviews, or promise response times. The source homepage’s architectural hero imagery is labeled as existing brand imagery, not represented as a verified completed project. The 3D rendering is labeled as a concept.
