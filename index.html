A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Where do you work?+

Creative Pro’s published service area includes Elgin, St. Charles, Geneva, Schaumburg, and surrounding Chicagoland suburbs. Share your town when you inquire to confirm availability for your location.

Can we talk before I have a full plan?+

Yes. The existing Creative Pro website offers a free consultation. Bring your ideas, inspiration, and questions; a finished design isn’t needed to start the conversation.

Can I see the design before construction?+

Creative Pro offers 3D design and rendering. Ask about including layouts, material options, and visualizations in your project scope.

How much will my remodel cost?+

The scope, existing conditions, material choices, and level of finish affect the cost. A conversation and site assessment help the team prepare a project-specific estimate. This website concept does not provide price quotes.

When can my project start?+

Timing depends on the scope, design decisions, material availability, and the team’s schedule. Share your preferred timeframe during the consultation so it can be discussed before committing to a start date.
